# 🧭 Story-first Blogging Principles (Personal Narrative Focus)

Use this when personal posts outperform technical ones. Prioritize human stories with clear insights and light humor.

## Structure (reusable outline)

1) Hook (1–2 sentences)
   - Relatable moment + 1 vivid detail. Optional light joke.

2) Context & story (3–6 sentences)
   - Where/when/who; real emotion; one small, concrete scene.

3) Turning point
   - The mentor/coach/event that flipped your perspective; the "aha" moment.

4) Key insights (1–2 points)
   - Say what you realized, plainly. Include one quoteable line.

5) Apply now (3–5 bullets)
   - Practical steps readers can use immediately.

6) Soft close + CTA
   - Return to the human angle. Invite a story or comment. Suggest subscribe.

## Voice & style

- Friendly, first-person (“mình/tui/I”).
- Short sentences, mobile-first spacing. Emojis sparingly, at emotional beats.
- Prefer everyday examples over heavy theory. Data/diagrams only when pivotal.

## LinkedIn promo template (for personal posts)

—
Hook (1 line): a sharp question or relatable tension.

3–4 short lines: context → turning point → 1-line insight (quoteable)

CTA (soft): “Viết chi tiết trong blog tuần này (link ở comment). Bạn từng gặp giống vậy chưa?”

Attachment: one image/meme/quote card from the post.
—

## Publish checklist

- One quoteable takeaway?
- 3–5 actionable bullets?
- Soft CTA at the end?
- Image/meme/quote card included?
- Newsletter subscribe link at end and author bio?

# Blogger Skill

This skill helps you write engaging and high-quality content across different formats. It provides structured frameworks for personal storytelling and technical engineering posts.

## Core Workflows

Choose the appropriate writing style based on your goal:

- **Personal Narrative**: Focuses on storytelling, human connection, and light humor. Best for building a personal brand and sharing life lessons.
  - See [personal_writing.md](references/personal_writing.md)
- **Technical Engineering**: Focuses on solving concrete problems with code, architecture, and evidence. Best for tutorials, documentation, and sharing engineering solutions.
  - See [technical_writing.md](references/technical_writing.md)
- **Research Deep Dives**: Specialized technical writing for comparing systems, analyzing semantics, or codifying evolving capabilities.
  - See [technical_writing.md#research-deep-dive-mode](references/technical_writing.md) (Section: Research Deep Dive Mode)

## General Principles

- **Platform Aware**: Always consider the target platform (e.g., LinkedIn vs. Personal Blog).
- **Actionable**: Ensure readers leave with something they can use or do.
- **Authentic**: Maintain a consistent voice that reflects your expertise and personality.
